---
name: grill-me
description: Stress-test a plan or design by walking every branch of the decision tree until reaching shared understanding.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Interview me relentlessly about aspects of this until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the decision tree, resolving dependencies between decisions. Be precise in your questions.

Ask sets of questions in non-blocking order. For each question, provide your recommended answer.

If a *fact* can be found by exploring the environment (filesystem, tools, etc.), look it up or verify it using subagents.

Do not act on it until I confirm we have reached a shared understanding.
